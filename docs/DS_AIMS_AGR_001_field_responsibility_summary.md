# DS AIMS AGR 001 Field Responsibility Summary

This summary identifies the remaining fields and actions after the agreed supplier information was added to the agreement. Blank items remain for the relevant party to complete.

## Digital Natives

### Legal and corporate

- Legal name, registration number, registered address, country of establishment, contact person and email.
- Authorised signatory name, title, signature and date.
- Annex E: confirm every person who will work on the System, their access held and evidence of intellectual-property assignment to Digital Natives.
- Annex E1: disclose parent, subsidiary, affiliate and associated undertakings; any relationship with OrionX or its affiliates; other engagements that could conflict; and how conflicts will be managed. State "none" where applicable.
- Annex E2: each named individual's undertaking, device declarations and signature.
- Confirm receipt and acknowledgement of the Data Sentinels policies referenced in clause 9 and clause 18.
- Obtain legal review of warranties, indemnity, liability, insurance and cyber-liability wording.

### Financial and commercial

- Confirm the invoicing schedule, invoice recipient and VAT treatment.
- Agree the liability cap, insurance requirements and cyber-liability terms.
- Confirm the commercial terms for the six-month operating period, if any.

### Other and technical

- Move the production Anthropic API key from the local env file into approved managed deployment secret storage, and rotate it if it has been exposed.
- Prepare the consolidated deployment procedure and operating runbook before handover.
- Maintain the build record, AI lifecycle records, component list, model/prompt/schema/configuration documentation and monthly monitoring notes.
- Implement and document low-confidence or incomplete-scan review flagging once Data Sentinels approves the threshold.
- Keep test receipts limited to receipts belonging to Digital Natives developers, with appropriate consent, minimisation and deletion/retention controls.
- Provide the agreed 30 days of defect support after acceptance.

## Data Sentinels

### Legal and privacy

- Confirm the Delivery Lead, Internal System Owner and receiving engineer.
- Approve Anthropic and Supabase as external service providers/sub-processors for the relevant data flows.
- Confirm approved processing/storage locations, retention periods, cross-border transfer basis and any required data-processing terms.
- Confirm whether the developers' real test receipts may be used, and record the applicable approval and retention position.
- Complete the lawful-basis, impact assessment, risk assessment, privacy notice and user access/correction/deletion/complaint route required before go-live.
- Record the approved AI services and coding assistant in the AI Tool Register.
- Confirm any legal amendments to confidentiality, warranties, indemnity, liability, insurance, cyber liability and termination.
- Complete authorised signatures and dates.

### Financial and commercial

- Confirm the invoicing schedule, invoice recipient and VAT treatment.
- Confirm acceptance milestones and the commercial treatment of the six-month operating period.
- Agree the liability cap, insurance requirements and cyber-liability terms.
- Confirm the retained-fee release conditions and any operating-period fees.

### Other and operational

- Provide the named receiving engineer and obtain their written deployment/operation confirmation at handover.
- Approve the low-confidence threshold and any additional acceptance metrics for the build record.
- Provide or approve the relevant AI-management, data-management, third-party and security policies.
- Review and counter-sign monthly monitoring notes during the operating period.
- Confirm go-live in writing only after the required legal, privacy, impact, risk and operational conditions are complete.
- Control the Anthropic, Supabase and deployment accounts and confirm the approved data locations and retention settings.
